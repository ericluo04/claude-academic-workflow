Example decks

Both decks, their figures, and the theme they render in were generated entirely with Claude Code, as a proof of concept for the claude-academic-workflow repository. The research content is invented for demonstration. Anything you generate with these tools is your responsibility to verify before relying on it.

  • Research talk: a seminar deck. Staged reveals, assertion titles, jump buttons into the appendix, a paginated reference list, and a progress bar that completes at the closing slide.
  • Teaching lecture: a classroom deck. Teaching blocks, worked examples, discussion prompts, agenda tracking, and the same staging and gate machinery.

Both decks render with the repository's starter theme: the machinery ships with one worked look, and the theme file marks every spot where your own taste replaces it.

Navigate with the arrow keys; press f for fullscreen and Esc for the slide overview. The sources are in the repository under examples/.
