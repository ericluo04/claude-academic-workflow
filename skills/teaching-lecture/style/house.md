# House style (stub)

Your house style lives here: your palette rationale, closing-slide wording,
density calibration, author line, button diction. The skill reads this file
for values; fill it in. The private original carried the author's own versions
of the sections below and is not published.

## The author line

`author: "Your Name"`, in the exact form you want on every title slide.

## Dates

The format sets `date-format: long`. Change it here if you want another form.

## The dark palette

Why your lecture palette is what it is: the ground, the body ink, the accent
family, and the contrast each pair measures. A designed dark palette beats an
inverted light one, because contrast gets chosen instead of computed. The
example deck's compiled CSS shows one worked answer; your values go here and
in your theme.

## Density calibration

Where a lecture sits relative to your talks. A lecture deck doubles as a study
document, so it runs wordier than a seminar deck; name the sample deck you
calibrate against.

## Closing-slide wording

The heading and register of your end-of-class slide (homework, next steps),
and where the one allowed aside goes.

## Button diction

Your diction for `.jump` labels; see the research-talk stub for a workable
default.

## Divider titles

House rules for section-divider titles, e.g. whether the title may repeat the
number the disc already shows.
